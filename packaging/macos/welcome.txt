QCBridgeA sends what QCView shows and plays out of an AJA device's SDI and
HDMI outputs, as a 10-bit RGB signal at the source's frame rate, with the
mixed audio embedded.

This installs QCBridgeA.app in /Applications and registers it with QCView
(/Library/Application Support/QCView/Outputs/aja.json). Afterwards, in
QCView, open the Output section and press Start on the AJA row: QCView
starts QCBridgeA, which takes the device and reports its format on the row.
Nothing is sent until Start.

AJA's own software for the device (AJA Desktop Software 17.x, for the
driver) and QCView 2.5.7 or later are required.
